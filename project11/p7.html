India Women Crowned Asia Cup Champions

India's women's cricket team defeated Sri Lanka by 72 runs to win the Women's Asia Cup, securing a record eighth title. The victory adds another major achievement to India's women's cricket history and gives the team strong momentum ahead of upcoming international competitions.

AI Investment Faces New Questions

Global technology stocks came under pressure as investors reacted to calls for a more measured approach to artificial-intelligence development. Concerns about the enormous investment required for AI infrastructure have added uncertainty to technology markets.

Indias's Tech Industry Looks Abroad

India's Finance Minister has proposed a dedicated forum to help Indian technology companies deal with foreign regulators as they expand internationally. The proposal focuses on areas including AI, cybersecurity and emerging digital technologies.

National Engineers' Day Approaches

India will observe National Engineers' Day on September 15 in honour of engineer and Bharat Ratna recipient M. Visvesvaraya. The day recognizes the contribution of engineers to India's infrastructure, industry and technological development.

India's Next Industrial Wave

India's technology and manufacturing landscape is expanding beyond traditional IT services. Space technology, semiconductors, data centres, electronics, solar manufacturing and aerospace are emerging as important sectors for future investment and industrial growth.

India Begins T20 Series With Big Win

India defeated Afghanistan by seven wickets in the first T20I in New Delhi. Abhishek Sharma produced a spectacular 82 from just 32 balls as India chased Afghanistan's 157-run target in only 13.4 overs.

A new chapter for Indian Cricket

For the first time in an Indian cricket match, both Vande Mataram and Jana Gana Mana were played before India's T20I against Afghanistan. The unusual pre-match ceremony attracted attention as India began its series in New Delhi.

Global Markets Watch AI and Oil

Global markets are facing renewed uncertainty as concerns about AI investment combine with rising energy-market pressures. Technology stocks have been volatile while geopolitical tensions continue to affect global oil supplies.
